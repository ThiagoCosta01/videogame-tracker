# Risks

Risks describe **uncertain events that may occur and negatively affect the project**.

## RISK-001 — Risk Name

**Description:** Describe the risk and its potential cause.

**Probability:** `Low | Medium | High`

**Impact:** `Low | Medium | High`

**Risk Level:** `Low | Medium | High | Critical`

**Mitigation:** Describe actions that reduce the probability or impact.

**Contingency:** Describe what will be done if the risk occurs.

**Owner:** Person or role responsible for monitoring the risk.
