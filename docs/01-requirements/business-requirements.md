# Business Requirements

Business requirements describe **what the business needs to achieve**, not how the software will implement it.

## BR-001 — Requirement Name

**Description:** Describe the business need.

**Rationale:** Explain why the business needs it.

**Expected Outcome:** Describe the desired business result.
